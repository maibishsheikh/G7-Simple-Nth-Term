# NthQuest — Story Art Brief (Grade 7)

## Setting & Aesthetic
- **Environment:** A bustling Singapore night market (pasar malam).
- **Time:** Evening/Night with warm paper lantern lights, festive fairy bulbs, colorful stall awnings.
- **Atmosphere:** Lively, educational, welcoming, vibrant community feel.
- **Food / Items:** Generic, halal-friendly, and culturally inclusive (satay skewers, steamed kueh, bubble tea cups, fruit skewers). **Strictly no pork or alcohol.**
- **Characters:**
  - **Jun Kai:** 13-year-old Singaporean Chinese boy, curious, energetic, wearing a casual polo/t-shirt.
  - **Meera:** 13-year-old Singaporean Indian girl, thoughtful, observant, wearing a casual jacket/t-shirt, carries a notebook/tablet.
  - **Singa the Lion Cub:** Friendly lion cub mascot with a cute mane, enthusiastic mentor.

---

### Panel 1: Stall Number Fifty
- **File:** `1.png`
- **Scene:** The street perspective of the night market. Stalls stretch into the distance with glowing stall numbers ("Stall 1", "Stall 2", "Stall 3").
- **Action:** A friendly customer is talking with Jun Kai and Meera at Stall 1, pointing far down the road toward Stall 50. Jun Kai looks puzzled at the long street, while Meera smiles as she begins calculating.
- **Key Details:** Visible stall number signboards and neatly displayed satay sticks (5 at stall 1, 8 at stall 2).

### Panel 2: n Is the Stall Number
- **File:** `2.png`
- **Scene:** Singa the Lion Cub is holding or standing next to an illuminated market formula signboard displaying `nth term = 3n + 2`.
- **Action:** Singa points to the letter **n** with a wooden pointer. Jun Kai points to the `3` and `2`, realizing his misconception, while Meera shows the substitution calculation on a chalk tablet: `3(1) + 2 = 5`.
- **Key Details:** Highlight that `3n` means `3 × n`, not "start at 3 and add 2".

### Panel 3: Building a Signboard
- **File:** `3.png`
- **Scene:** A stall workshop table. A comparison chart is overlaid on the night-market backdrop showing the sequence `4, 7, 10, 13…` aligned against the 3 times table `3, 6, 9, 12…`.
- **Action:** Meera shows how each stock number is shifted up by 1 (+1 offset). Singa holds up two fingers to emphasize checking **both** Stall 1 and Stall 2 before trusting any formula.
- **Key Details:** Clear visual offset (+1) and double-check checkmarks for `n = 1` and `n = 2`.

### Panel 4: Stall Fifty, Solved!
- **File:** `4.png`
- **Scene:** Jun Kai and Meera proudly hand the customer a freshly marked formula board: `Stall 50 = 3(50) + 2 = 152 satay sticks`.
- **Action:** The customer is delighted. Singa gives a proud lion paw stamp of approval with golden confetti and lantern glows.
- **Key Details:** Clear celebration of problem-solving without walking 50 stalls!
